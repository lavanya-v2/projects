How DSR Health Status Works

A guide for team leads on how the Red / Amber / Green status is calculated

Overview

The daily health status is 100% data-driven from your Jira board. AI writes the report narrative but cannot change the health color. A separate manager escalation path exists but can only make things worse, never better.

1 Axis 1: Blocked Tickets

Checks what percentage of your Work-In-Progress tickets have been blocked for more than 3 days.

ConditionScoreMeaning
Long-term blocked % below threshold (default 20%) 0 No blocking concern
Long-term blocked % at or above threshold 1 Too many tickets stuck
What counts as WIP: In Progress, In Review, In QA, Reopened.
Excluded: UAT, Pending Deployment, Backlog, Done.

2 Axis 2: Production Bugs

Counts fresh production bugs (Bug/Sub-bug with Environment = Production, created within 15 days of sprint start).

What is excluded from scoring

  • Old/planned bugs (created >15 days before sprint) — these are intentional remediation work
  • Re-opened sub-bugs against active stories — tracked but not penalized

Scoring Rules

Unaddressed Bugs (TODO / In Progress)Score
None, or 1 Low-priority only0
1 bug (Medium or High priority)1
1 High-priority, or 2+ bugs, or 3+ of any priority2
Addressed Bugs (in QA / Review / UAT)Score
Any count — team is already working on themCapped at 1
Spillover Bugs (carried from prior sprint)Score
Any spillover bugs detectedForces at least 1
Final bug score = max(unaddressed score, addressed score, spillover score)

3 Axis 3: Sprint Burndown Scrum Only

Compares how far the sprint has elapsed vs. how much work is done. Skipped for Kanban projects.

How "off-track %" is calculated

offTrackPct = max(0, sprintElapsedPct - completionPct)
Tickets in QA count as 0.6 toward completion (partial credit for late-stage work).

ConditionScoreMeaning
Sprint is less than 20% elapsed0Too early to judge
Off-track % is below 30%0On track
Off-track % is 30% – 50%1At risk
Off-track % is above 50%2Critical

Manager Insights Escalation (Optional Layer)

Managers can add written insights about things not visible in Jira (customer complaints, team issues, etc.). AI analyzes the text and may suggest escalating the health.

⚠️
Escalation only — insights can move Green → Amber or Amber → Red, but can never downgrade (make things look better than the data says).

What triggers an escalation suggestion

  • Customer escalations or complaints
  • Team attrition risk or HR concerns
  • Delivery blockers not captured in Jira
  • Stakeholder dissatisfaction

What the user sees

Effective Health = max( Computed , Insights )  =  AMBER

The UI shows both signals with a tooltip when escalation is active. If insights are updated and no escalation is needed, the override auto-clears.

Weekly & Account Roll-Up

Weekly Project Health
Majority voting across daily health values:
  • >50% of days Red → RED
  • Any Red or Amber day → AMBER
  • All days Green → GREEN
Account Health
Threshold-based across projects:
  • Red project % ≥ threshold (default 30%) → RED
  • Any Red or Amber project → AMBER
  • All projects Green → GREEN

Key Takeaways for Team Leads

  • Health is fully data-driven — it comes from your Jira board, not AI opinion
  • AI writes the summary text but the health color is locked by the algorithm
  • Unblock tickets promptly — anything blocked >3 days hurts Axis 1
  • Address production bugs fast — even moving a bug to QA reduces its score impact
  • Sprint pacing matters — if completion falls 30%+ behind elapsed time, it triggers Amber
  • Manager escalation is additive only — no one can make a Red look Green
  • Each day stands alone — tomorrow's health is recalculated fresh from Jira state
